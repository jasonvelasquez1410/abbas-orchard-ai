💡 Admissions & CADO Workflow Guide

Frequently Asked Questions & Coordinator Direct Routing

How our 24/7 AI Admissions Digital Employee guides parents and seamlessly connects them with Campus Admissions & Development Officers (CADO).

📞

How do parents connect directly with a Campus CADO (e.g., CDO Alwana)?

When a parent asks to contact a coordinator (e.g., CDO Alwana, Bukidnon, BGC, Cebu, Davao), the AI instantly provides their direct mobile/hotline ((0917) 707 2668) and dedicated email (alwana@theabbasorchard.edu.ph). If the parent leaves their name and phone number, the AI automatically dispatches an immediate callback alert directly to the Alwana CADO desk.

Test in AI Chat: "Contact CDO Alwana Coordinator" →
💬

How does human handoff work on the official Facebook Page?

When the Facebook Page is connected with Admin access, Meta's native Handover Protocol automatically flags parent inquiries requesting staff assistance as "Unread / Needs Human Attention" inside Meta Business Suite. School administrators and CADO coordinators receive push notifications and can reply directly in the chat at any time.

View Facebook Messenger Demo →
📅

How are Montessori Observation Walkthroughs scheduled?

Montessori observation tours occur during authentic weekday morning work cycles (8:00 AM – 11:30 AM). The AI collects parent date preferences, child's age/level, and chosen campus, formatting a confirmed booking dispatch to the target campus admissions calendar.

Test in AI Chat: "Book a Walkthrough" →
🌐

What languages does the Admissions AI speak?

The AI is multi-lingual and culturally attuned: it fluently converses in English, Bisaya / Cebuano (for CDO, Bukidnon, Davao, and Cebu families), and Tagalog (for Luzon campuses), answering questions warmly and naturally in the parent's preferred language.

Try asking in Bisaya or Tagalog →
